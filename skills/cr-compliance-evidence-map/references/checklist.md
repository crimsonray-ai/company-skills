# Compliance Evidence Map: interpretation checklist

Use this glossary to interpret the supplied records. It is not a vendor specification, a substitute for the customer's policy, or evidence about a real environment.

## Terms and evidence expectations

| Term or control area | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Design evidence | The documented intended control, responsibility, or process. |
| Implementation evidence | Evidence that the control has been configured or put in place. |
| Operating evidence | Records showing relevant control activity during the assessment period. |
| Partial support | Evidence supports only part of the requirement, scope, or period. |
| Not applicable | An explicit, reviewable applicability decision with a supported rationale. |

## Synthetic example

The following is an invented training example, not a finding to copy into a report:

An access-review policy describes quarterly reviews, but no completed review records are supplied for the assessment period. Map the policy to design evidence and leave operating effectiveness unestablished.

## Reviewer questions

- Every control identifier comes from the supplied versioned control set.
- Each mapping explains what the evidence supports and what it does not.
- Evidence freshness and sample limitations are recorded.
- No preparation status is presented as certification or a final audit opinion.

## Handoff record

When another analyst continues the work, preserve the assessed scope, observation window, source references, unresolved questions, and the decision owner. Keep this library's files unchanged; working evidence and completed reports belong in the user's approved workspace.
