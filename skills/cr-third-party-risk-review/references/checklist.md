# Third-Party Risk Review: interpretation checklist

Use this glossary to interpret the supplied records. It is not a vendor specification, a substitute for the customer's policy, or evidence about a real environment.

## Terms and evidence expectations

| Term or control area | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Supplier assertion | A self-reported statement requiring the appropriate degree of corroboration. |
| Independent assessment | A third-party report with its own period, scope, exceptions, and reliance limits. |
| Contract commitment | An agreed obligation; not proof of operational performance. |
| Operational evidence | Records of the relevant control or service operating within a stated period. |
| Acceptance condition | A requirement the decision owner may impose before or during use. |

## Synthetic example

The following is an invented training example, not a finding to copy into a report:

A supplier provides an assessment for its hosting platform, while the purchase includes a separate analytics service. Record the scope mismatch and request evidence for the purchased service rather than treating the report as universal coverage.

## Reviewer questions

- The assessed service and evidence scope match or their differences are stated.
- Each risk finding identifies its evidence type and limitations.
- Follow-up questions are tied to a decision, not a generic questionnaire dump.
- Residual-risk acceptance has a named role or an explicit ownership gap.

## Handoff record

When another analyst continues the work, preserve the assessed scope, observation window, source references, unresolved questions, and the decision owner. Keep this library's files unchanged; working evidence and completed reports belong in the user's approved workspace.
