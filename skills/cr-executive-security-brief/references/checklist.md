# Executive Security Brief: interpretation checklist

Use this glossary to interpret the supplied records. It is not a vendor specification, a substitute for the customer's policy, or evidence about a real environment.

## Terms and evidence expectations

| Term or control area | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Risk statement | The plausible business effect, its supporting evidence, and its uncertainty. |
| Metric | A defined measure with population, period, and known collection limitations. |
| Trend | A comparison of measures whose definitions and coverage are consistent. |
| Decision request | A concrete choice, its owner, and the consequence of delay. |
| Delivery progress | Completed work supported by evidence, separated from planned work. |

## Synthetic example

The following is an invented training example, not a finding to copy into a report:

A report shows fewer open findings after one business unit stopped reporting. Describe the coverage change; do not present the lower count as a verified improvement in risk.

## Reviewer questions

- Every headline claim is traceable to the underlying assessment.
- Metrics include their denominator and time/scope definition.
- Financial or trend claims use supplied assumptions and comparable evidence.
- Each requested decision names an owner or states that ownership is unresolved.

## Handoff record

When another analyst continues the work, preserve the assessed scope, observation window, source references, unresolved questions, and the decision owner. Keep this library's files unchanged; working evidence and completed reports belong in the user's approved workspace.
